GOOD TO KNOW

Frequently asked
questions.

If you need more clarity, contact us for a private discussion.

Will my enquiry remain confidential?+

We treat enquiries discreetly and limit the use of personal information to what is reasonably needed to assess and manage your request, subject to applicable law.

How much do investigation services cost?+

Fees depend on the scope, complexity, location and time involved. Contact us for a confidential quote; no price is assumed before discussing your requirements.

How long does an investigation take?+

Timing varies with the type of enquiry, available information and circumstances. We will discuss a realistic estimate after understanding the case.

Are your methods legal?+

Services are offered only within applicable Indian law. We do not provide phone tapping, hacking, unauthorised account/device access, trespass or other illegal methods.

Can you collect evidence for a legal matter?+

We may help organise lawfully obtained factual information. Whether material is admissible or useful in a legal proceeding is a matter for your lawyer and the relevant court.

Will I receive a report?+

Where appropriate to the agreed scope, findings can be summarised in a clear report. The format and deliverables are discussed before work begins.

Which areas do you serve?+

We are based in Mira Road East and can discuss enquiries in Mira Road, Thane, Mumbai and other locations depending on scope and feasibility.

How do I get started?+

Call, WhatsApp or use the enquiry form. Share only the information necessary for an initial discussion; avoid sending highly sensitive documents until a secure process is agreed.
