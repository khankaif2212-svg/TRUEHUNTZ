Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

TrueHurtz Investigators (“we”, “us” or “our”) respects the privacy of people who contact us. This policy describes how information submitted through our website or shared during an enquiry may be handled. It is a general website policy and should be reviewed for your actual business practices before publication.

1. Information you provide

When you contact us, you may choose to provide your name, phone number, email address, requested service and the message you send. Please share only information reasonably necessary for an initial enquiry. Do not submit passwords, financial account credentials, identity documents or highly sensitive material through the general website form.

2. How information may be used

Information may be used to respond to your enquiry, discuss whether a requested service is appropriate and lawful, provide a quote, communicate about a case if you engage us, maintain necessary business records, prevent misuse and comply with legal obligations.

3. Confidentiality and disclosure

We aim to handle enquiries discreetly and limit access to information to people who need it for the stated purpose. We do not promise absolute secrecy where disclosure is required by law, court order or a legitimate legal obligation, or is necessary to protect rights and safety.

4. Third-party services

The website may use Formspree to process contact-form submissions, Google Maps to display a map and Google Fonts to load typography. These providers may process technical data under their own terms and privacy policies. Review the relevant providers' policies before enabling those services.

5. Cookies and technical data

This static website does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies. Hosting providers may process standard technical logs such as IP address, browser type and request time for security and operation. If analytics, tracking or additional third-party tools are added later, this policy should be updated.

6. Retention and security

Information should be retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose collected, applicable recordkeeping needs and legal obligations. We take reasonable steps to limit access, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to ask about information you have submitted or request correction or deletion where applicable. Requests may be subject to verification and legal retention requirements. Contact: truehurtz27@gmail.com.

8. Children

This website is intended for adults seeking information about investigation services. Do not submit a child's personal information unless you are authorised to do so and it is necessary and lawful.

9. Updates

We may update this policy as our practices or legal requirements change. The latest version will be posted on this page with a revised date.
